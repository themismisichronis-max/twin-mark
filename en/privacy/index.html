Privacy policy · updated 17 November 2026

What we do with your details

In short: only what you write in the form, only to reply to you. We sell nothing and use no advertising cookies.

Who we are

TWINMARK is a project run by two siblings in Athens (not a company). For anything about your data, write to hello@twinmark.gr.

What we collect and why

  • The Brief form. Name, business or brand, optionally your Instagram or TikTok, what you need, budget, message and an email or phone number. We use them to reply and send you a proposal, because you asked us to (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Visit statistics. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not follow you to other sites.
  • What your browser remembers. A small note in the tab's memory (sessionStorage) so the logo intro plays once. It is cleared when you close the tab and never leaves your device.

Who else sees it

  • Formspree receives the form and forwards it to us. It is a US company, so form details may be transferred outside the EU. Its safeguards are described in its own policy.
  • Cloudflare hosts the site and counts the statistics.
  • WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok only if you tap a link to them; from there their own terms apply.

Nothing from third parties loads before you tap it, and we pass your details to no one else.

How long we keep it

As long as we need to talk. If we don't start working together, we delete the brief at the latest 12 months after our last contact.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you, or ask us to stop using it. Write to hello@twinmark.gr and we reply within a month. If you think we handled it wrongly, you can complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority.
