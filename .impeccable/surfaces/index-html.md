# Surface: TWINMARK home (index.html, en/index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: Athens local-business owner, usually on a phone, deciding whether these two siblings "get" their kind of place. Action: WhatsApp message or the 4-step brief (equal weight). Proof: real reels and posts with real numbers (479K Lizzy's reel, 89K Xagk), the Lizzy's website. Constraints: Greek primary + English page; must stay smooth on iOS Safari and mid-range Android; no page-tall scrubbed SVG, no scroll-spawned images, no large blurs/backdrop-filter, no iframe embeds; content readable without JS. The user explicitly asked that the old "line that draws itself" survive, lighter.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a ride on the TWINMARK line. Scrolling is travelling: services are stations, platforms are interchanges, the brief is the terminus. Refuses the dark agency hero with a generic headline over a reel grid and service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Athens Metro wayfinding translated to the brand. Station-tile white ground; indigo station signs (white Greek name, smaller Latin beneath; the sign is the section heading) mounted on white rings on one line drawn in the logo gradient (aqua → steel → indigo → teal); the logo mark as the line badge; octilinear routing on the network map; amber LED figures only for live numbers, on a row-based departure board; teal for the primary action. Geologica throughout, heavy for signs and display. Structure is the only ornament on the work itself; one figure per piece of work; a strict module row for the photos; one quiet held passage after the reels.

STORY: Visitor sees the network map of the whole studio, rides into three dedicated stations (Video in phones, Photos as a passing strip, Websites in a browser), believes the numbers, ends at the terminus and sends a brief or a WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: White header with the full-colour logo, station links with "you are here" rings, EN, teal "Send a brief". Left: heavy headline «Δύο αδέρφια. Ένα σήμα. Περιεχόμενο που σε σταματάει.», one line of sub, WhatsApp (teal) + brief side by side, the departure board below. Right: the TWINMARK network map card that draws itself, every stop a link. The page line leaves from a departure ring at the foot of the hero into Βίντεο.

FORM: Athens Metro line diagram (my top-ranked grounded candidate, #1 of 7; chosen as IMPECCABLE'S PICK over the assigned Periptero). Seed key bbe9c589.

MOTION: Metaphor: a train running the line. Travel is vertical and smooth (signature ease "brake": quick departure, long gentle stop); arrivals are lateral and crisp (ease "doors": fast centre-split clip reveal). Moves: the map draws stop by stop; line segments fill per section by transform scaleY scrub; station rings light as the tip passes; station signs and the website open like doors; reels pull in laterally; the photos pass sideways on a sticky stage while the page scrolls down (desktop and phone, lighter scroll length on phones); amber figures count up once. Still: body copy, the brief form (only its step change moves), footer. Phones: same line in a narrow gutter, same stations and doors, reels in a native swipe row, the map waits until it is on screen. Reduced motion / no JS: line fully drawn, stations lit, map drawn, photos as a native swipe row, everything visible.

AMENDMENTS (user decisions after the first prototype, 2026-10-22): (1) photos and videos never mix; each has its own station and its own presentation, so the first-viewport work train was replaced by the network map. (2) "More mature, between the old site and the plain prototype": a white header with the colour logo instead of an indigo bar; static atmospheric fields (soft brand-colour radial washes in hero, Video night ground and Photos mist; a teal-to-indigo gradient terminus panel); the map shown on a raised card; the scroll-driven photo strip kept on phones. The user approved this render ("Ναι, μου αρέσει"). (3) Type: Geologica replaces the planned Sofia Sans; chosen on a Greek-glyph comparison for signage clarity, shipped and approved.
