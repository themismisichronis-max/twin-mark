# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no framework and no build step (confirmed 2026-10-22). Must deploy as plain files to any static host (Cloudflare, Netlify, etc.). Contact form posts to Formspree (`https://formspree.io/f/xnjednyq`).

## Users

Owners and managers of local businesses in Athens (cafés and F&B first, also beauty, wellness/physio, hospitality) who are deciding whether to hand their Instagram/TikTok to an outside team. They usually arrive from TikTok/Instagram or a referral, on a phone, and judge in seconds whether this team "gets" their kind of place. Secondary: the same visitors on desktop when they sit down to compare options or send a brief.

## Product Purpose

The site is TWINMARK's own shop window. It must show real work that performed, explain the three services, and turn a curious owner into a sent brief or a WhatsApp/phone conversation. Success = briefs and direct contacts from local businesses.

## Positioning

A two-person studio run by siblings ("TWIN") that makes everything in-house: scripting, shooting, editing, designing and posting. Small enough that the owner talks to the people who make the content; proven by real local results (a Lizzy's reel at 479K views, Xagk TikTok at 89K).

## Operating Context

- Services: (01) short-form video for Reels & TikTok, (02) static posts / art-directed feed design, (03) websites (example: the Lizzy's Coffee & more site, live at https://lizzys-site-test.themismisichronis.workers.dev). Also captions, content planning, full social management.
- Contact paths: 4-step brief form (who you are → services → monthly budget range → contact/message) delivered via Formspree; WhatsApp +30 694 403 6657; phone +30 698 376 0254; email hello@twinmark.gr; Instagram @twinmark_.
- Budget ranges offered in the brief: Under €300, €300–€600, €600–€1,000, €1,000+, Let's discuss.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual: Greek is the primary language, with an English version reachable from a language switch (confirmed 2026-10-22).
- Must run smoothly on iOS Safari and mid-range Android. The previous site stuttered everywhere; the root causes were a page-tall scroll-scrubbed SVG line with constant geometry rebuilds, images spawned and animated on every scroll step, large blur filters and backdrop-filters, a live iframe embed, and four videos. None of those patterns may return.
- Content readable without JavaScript; reduced-motion version complete.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: TWINMARK (wordmark set as TWIN / MARK, with ®).
- Logo mark: a square frame containing a V and a vertical stem (T + M), stroked in the brand gradient. Files: `assets/img/logo-mark.png`, `logo-full.png`, `logo-transparent.png`; sources in `NOT-FOR-UPLOAD/`.
- Brand gradient: aqua #7ACFD6 → steel #5384B7 → indigo #3B50A3 (wordmark colour #3D51A3) → teal #007775.
- Voice (from CLAUDE.md): warm, casual, short clear sentences, no corporate dryness, no clickbait or exaggeration, clear CTAs, respect for the Athens/neighbourhood context.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work first; real posts and real numbers carry the pitch.
2. Speak like the neighbourhood, not like an agency deck.
3. Every screen should leave one obvious next step: send a brief or message on WhatsApp.
4. Fast and steady on the phone a café owner actually carries, or it doesn't ship.
